Vuzol

Private peer-to-peer messenger for Android. No accounts, no phone number, no central server.

• Offline Bluetooth mesh — talk nearby with no internet
• End-to-end encrypted (Noise protocol) private chats and groups
• Photos, videos and files up to 50 MB
• Location channels and Nostr for online conversations

Transport

Four connection layers. Every layer works without a central server.

Layer 01

Bluetooth network

live

A decentralized, self-healing mesh over Bluetooth LE. Every device is both a sender and a relay, so coverage grows with every peer. Works completely offline — no towers, no Wi-Fi, no internet.

30 m per hop · multi-hop mesh no internet Noise encryption
Layer 02

WiFi Direct

coming soon

Direct device-to-device WiFi without a router or internet. Far more bandwidth and range than Bluetooth, which makes it the right layer for big files and larger groups in the field.

up to 200 m high bandwidth no router, no internet
Layer 03

P2P network

live

Direct libp2p connections between phones over the internet. Peer discovery over DHT, topics for group chats, no server in the middle.

direct peer-to-peer group topics
Layer 04

Nostr relays

live

Public relays carry location channels and long-distance conversations. Relays never learn who talks to whom: every channel uses its own key.

long distance channels

What's new

  • The chat list looks like Telegram: the folders all, unread, groups and people, a side menu with the profile, the calls and the settings, and a separate tab with the history of the calls.
  • The menu of a message is compact now: a small grid of buttons over the chat, the long press works on the whole message and not only on the time of the message.
  • A channel that is opened from a link keeps its own name, the name of the place is not shown instead of the name of the group.
  • Channels and private chats where you wrote stay in the chat list, they do not disappear after you leave them.
  • Deleted messages do not come back with the next message of the chat.
  • The offline room is remembered and shows up in the chat list, you can leave it right there.
  • The landing page and the README are updated automatically with every release.
